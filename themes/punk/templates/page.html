In April 2025, labor and civil rights organizations held a town hall because Barrett refused to. Over 2,000 constituents signed a petition demanding he meet them in person. He sent a phone call instead. His absence was met with a chorus of boos. (source: Michigan Advance, April 23, 2025)

>> THE VOTING RECORD

SNAP CUTS Voted for a budget reconciliation bill containing the largest cuts to food assistance in American history. More than 20,000 people in MI-07 are at risk of losing SNAP benefits. 1.5 million Michiganders affected statewide. (source: GovTrack; Michigan Advance)
MEDICAID Voted to cut $880 billion from Medicaid. An estimated 23,000 people in MI-07 are projected to lose health coverage entirely in 2026. Three major insurers are already exiting Michigan's ACA marketplace, forcing 200,000 residents to find new plans. (source: Detroit News; Michigan Public)
TARIFFS Voted against a resolution to strike down emergency tariffs on Canada. The tariffs raise costs for families, small businesses, and supply chains across the district. (source: GovTrack)
VOTER DATA Signed a letter — originating from his own office — urging Michigan's Secretary of State to provide voter registration data to the Justice Department in connection with Detroit ballots. (source: Michigan Advance)
WAR POWERS Voted against a bipartisan resolution to limit military action against Iran without congressional approval. Later introduced a softer version with a 5-year sunset clause. (source: Detroit News, April 22, 2026)
