>> THE VOTING RECORD

SNAP CUTS Voted yes on a budget bill containing the largest cuts to food assistance in American history. More than 20,000 people in MI-07 are at risk of losing SNAP benefits. 1.5 million Michiganders affected statewide.
MEDICAID Voted to cut $880 billion from Medicaid. An estimated 23,000 people in MI-07 are projected to lose health coverage in 2026. Three major insurers — HAP CareSource, Molina Healthcare, and Physicians Health Plan — are already exiting Michigan's ACA marketplace, forcing 200,000 residents to find new plans.
TARIFFS Voted against a resolution to terminate emergency tariffs on Canada. The tariffs raise costs for families, small businesses, and supply chains across the district.
VOTER DATA Signed a letter urging Michigan's Secretary of State to hand voter registration data to the Justice Department in connection with Detroit ballots — in a state where officials found 15 instances of voter fraud out of 5.7 million votes cast in 2024.
WAR POWERS Voted against a bipartisan resolution to require congressional authorization before military action against Iran. Later introduced a softer version with a 5-year sunset — after the damage was done. (Detroit News, April 22, 2026)
